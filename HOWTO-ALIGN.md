# HOWTO — claim CEK alignment

This is a **how-to**. One outcome: claim CEK alignment. It does not restate axioms, does not copy kill criteria, and is not a second test suite.

---

## Outcome

You can say whether an implementation may claim CEK alignment.

## Steps

1. Open [KILL.md](KILL.md). That file is the gate.
2. Walk [Instant disqualification](KILL.md#instant-disqualification). A hit means you may not claim alignment — read the criterion there, not here.
3. Walk [Soft fail](KILL.md#soft-fail). A hit means the claim is not CEK-compatible until fixed — read the criterion there, not here.
4. For the conformance families named by K14, open [LAW.md §21 Conformance](LAW.md#21-conformance). Do not treat this page as a second suite.

Changing kill criteria is an amendment: [HOWTO-AMEND.md](HOWTO-AMEND.md).
