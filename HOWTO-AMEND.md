# HOWTO — amend law

This is a **how-to**. One outcome: amend law. Process and log live in [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md). This page does not restate axioms.

---

## Outcome

A law change is classified, processed, and recorded in the amendment log — or you learn it is editorial and not an amendment.

## Steps

1. Open [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md).
2. Classify the change: [May change without amendment](CHARTER.md#may-change-without-amendment) or [Requires amendment](CHARTER.md#requires-amendment). Editorial clarity that does not change meaning is not an amendment — that distinction lives in CHARTER.md, not here.
3. If it requires amendment, follow [Amendment process](CHARTER.md#amendment-process) in listed order (including the log step).
4. Confirm the record in the [Amendment log](CHARTER.md#amendment-log).

Shipping an implementation does not amend this law. Alignment claims use [HOWTO-ALIGN.md](HOWTO-ALIGN.md).
